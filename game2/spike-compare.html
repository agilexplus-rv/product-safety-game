Procedural vs CC0 GLTF - living room spike

Two versions of the same living room from Safety Detective. Open each in a new tab, screenshot both from the same angle, and judge which visual direction to take for the whole house.

A

Procedural room

LatheGeometry / ExtrudeGeometry / TubeGeometry, canvas-generated normal maps, a real PointLight with PCFSoftShadowMap, and a PMREMGenerator + RoomEnvironment env map. No external assets.
B

GLTF room

Kenney Furniture Kit 2.0 (CC0) loaded via THREE.GLTFLoader. Same lighting and room shell as the procedural version for a fair comparison.
What to look for: silhouette fidelity of the furniture (turned legs, sofa folds), how the light interacts with the surfaces (reflections on glossy parts, shadow softness), and the loading weight / asset footprint. The procedural room is fully self-contained (zero network fetches beyond the JS). The GLTF room fetches about 9 small GLB files totalling under 500 KB. Asset provenance is documented in game2/SPIKE-ASSET-LICENSES.md.
